### Learning: Already a (Todo) iOS developer
TODO apps are some of the basic apps found in nature, however they can teach so much, I can't explain. As a senior developer I so hated this type of apps, and thought they are teaching nothing. But, now as a beginner, I'm starting to see something valuable in this way of learning through making a **todo** app. I learnt about CRUD oprtation, of which i've yet to do the D. But from viewmodels to observables, this app has taught me a lot. Although putting the code here doesn't make sense, I will be uploading the working video as well as here is the [github link](https://github.com/saugatp/todos) to my work so far. 

### Resources and materials
Although I've found multitude of resource and tutorials while searching, only few of them seem to have any quality to them. So, I will be listing out the resources that I learnt from(and scraped the code from), for anyone reading this as well as myself. Here they are
- Peter Friese's [Todo App Tutorial](https://peterfriese.github.io/MakeItSo/tutorials/makeitso/01-building-a-simple-todo-list-ui/) as well as [his website](https://peterfriese.dev) are some gems.
- Matteo Manferdini's [SwiftUI and iOS Fundamentals](https://matteomanferdini.com/swift-parse-json/)
- Apple's basic on [SwiftUI](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/swiftui/building-lists-and-navigation)
- Paul Hudson's [100 days of Swift/UI](https://www.hackingwithswift.com/100/swiftui)
- And some youtube tutorials from [Sean Allen](https://www.youtube.com/@seanallen)

For my next project on IOS, I'm thinking about using the TFL api to get next buses and trains from the current location. Although there are multitude of apps, mine will be for learning.
